How I Work.

The same four steps, whether it's a three-day off-site or a seven-year program.

01
Define the ambition

Purpose, goals and what success looks like, agreed up front and measurable. I ask the questions that surface what's already known and what's still waiting to be asked.

02
Design the experience

Strategy, flow and every touchpoint, designed around how we want people to feel and what they should leave with. Budget, partners and the thousand details in between, with stakeholders aligned at every stage.

03
Lead end-to-end

From the first partner brief to the final goodbye, I hold the thread and direct every partner, so the time, budget and attention invested pay off.

04
Learn + evolve

Outcomes assessed against the original goals, a structured retrospective, and lessons carried forward so every program builds on the last.

Hi, I'm Reyna

I've always been a host, fascinated by what makes experiences great and obsessed with the details.

I deepened my love of hospitality in New York's top restaurants and nightclubs, where excellence was the baseline. I built an eco-tourism company in Mexico, designing tours you couldn't find anywhere else. And I've gathered founders and investors from around the world on a private island in Malaysia to take on pressing global challenges. The ingredients have always been the same: care about people, nail the details and make it memorable.

My background spans program leadership, employee experience design, brand and creative direction, space design and production. I've set the standards that agencies and vendors across six countries delivered to, and balanced a program's identity with global partners like Barclays, Accenture and Pearson. I think of myself equally as a creative and a doer, and this work sits exactly at that intersection.

The through-line across all of it is the same: a belief that great experiences are never the product of one big gesture. They're the accumulation of many small, deliberate ones, and that accumulation is what I'm here to build.

Let's build something together.

My first glimpse behind a production of a memorable event was at the age of 19 when I was a PA at a Rolling Stones’ concert in Madison Square Garden in New York City.

I knew immediately that I wanted to produce events that created memories worth repeating in stories told years later.

With my communication skills honed at New York University and the City itself creating in me a deep appreciation of diverse people, style, culture, art and how connectivity builds community, I have built a career producing extraordinary events worldwide that reflect what I have learned and what I love doing.

My experience spans 15 years across 4 continents with events staged on remote islands in IndonesiaMalaysia to 1,000 people on a mountainside eating dinner at one long, continuous table.

Whether staged in extraordinary surroundings or a more intimate event in London’s eastside, the goal is always connection and stretching the relationships beyond the last meal, activity or meeting.

Each new event is a challenge to make it even more memorable than the last one.

And I love a challenge!
